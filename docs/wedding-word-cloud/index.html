A wedding word cloud from every guest

Put one link on the invitation, the wedding website or a small card at each table. Guests add three words about the couple, and the cloud grows through the day.

Show it during the toasts, and keep it afterwards: it's the guest book in the words of the people who were there.

Start one now — free, no app

Takes ten seconds. Nobody needs an account.

How it works

  1. Start it. Name it after the person and the occasion, like "Sam & Jordan's wedding".
  2. Share the link in the wedding website, the invitation, a QR code on each table.
  3. Everyone adds three words from their phone or computer. Answers are anonymous, and each person gets one set.
  4. Watch it grow. You can hide any word you don't want and close the cloud when you're done.

Words people often choose

joyful, adventurous, inseparable, kind, fun, devoted, hilarious, meant-to-be, warm, generous, playful, loving, loyal, radiant, home, team, magic, sweet, grounded, wild, gentle, balanced, forever, perfect

The best clouds mix the obvious with the specific. Encourage people to pick the words they'd actually use about this person, not the safest ones.

Tips

  • Start it within a month of the day: clouds started on the website stop taking new words after 30 days.
  • Print the link as a QR code on table cards. Guests answer in seconds from their phones.
  • Have someone put it on the screen before the toasts, while it's still growing.
  • Keep it kind. Clouds meant to embarrass or hurt someone get removed, and anyone can ask for a cloud about them to be taken down.
