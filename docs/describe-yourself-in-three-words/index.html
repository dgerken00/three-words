How to describe yourself in three words

It comes up more often than you'd think: a job interview, a dating profile, a team icebreaker, the first slide of an "about me." Three words, no room to hide. Here's how to choose well — and a way to cheat honestly by finding out what other people would say.

The three-slot method

Strong answers rarely use three synonyms ("driven, motivated, ambitious" says one thing three times). Fill three different slots instead:

1. How you work — your reliable professional gear.
thorough · decisive · resourceful · organized · persistent

2. How you relate — what it's like to be around you.
warm · direct · patient · encouraging · loyal

3. What's distinct — the word only some people earn.
curious · unflappable · playful · candid · inventive

75 words that work

For interviews: adaptable, analytical, calm, collaborative, conscientious, decisive, dependable, diligent, disciplined, driven, efficient, meticulous, motivated, organized, persistent, practical, proactive, resourceful, self-aware, steady, strategic, thorough, versatile, focused, reliable

For character: honest, generous, loyal, patient, humble, fair, kind, principled, sincere, thoughtful, considerate, gracious, grounded, dependable, openhearted, candid, respectful, gentle, steadfast, warm, empathetic, forgiving, direct, trustworthy, genuine

For personality: curious, playful, witty, adventurous, optimistic, energetic, creative, observant, easygoing, spirited, imaginative, funny, bold, quirky, enthusiastic, calm, intense, wry, cheerful, inventive, spontaneous, thoughtful, lively, mellow, magnetic

Two traps

Words you can't back up. Every word invites the follow-up "tell me about a time you were ___." Pick words with a story attached.

Words you wish were true. Interviewers and friends alike can smell an aspiration dressed as a description. Which raises the real question…
