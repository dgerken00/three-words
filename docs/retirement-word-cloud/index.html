A retirement word cloud from everyone who worked with them

Thirty years of colleagues, one link. Everyone adds three words about the person who's retiring, and the words build into a live word cloud: the ones chosen again and again grow largest.

Show it during the speeches, print it in the card, or just hand them the phone. It says what a long career meant to the people who shared it.

Start one now — free, no app

Takes ten seconds. Nobody needs an account.

How it works

  1. Start it. Name it after the person and the occasion, like "Pat's retirement".
  2. Share the link in the team chat, an email to old colleagues, the department newsletter.
  3. Everyone adds three words from their phone or computer. Answers are anonymous, and each person gets one set.
  4. Watch it grow. You can hide any word you don't want and close the cloud when you're done.

Words people often choose

dependable, generous, steady, mentor, funny, patient, wise, calm, loyal, kind, unflappable, legendary, organized, fair, encouraging, hardworking, humble, sharp, warm, inspiring, trusted, witty, thoughtful, devoted

The best clouds mix the obvious with the specific. Encourage people to pick the words they'd actually use about this person, not the safest ones.

Tips

  • Send the link about a week before the send-off, and once more two days before. Most answers come in on the first day.
  • Start it no more than a month ahead: clouds started on the website stop taking new words after 30 days.
  • Close it the morning of the party so the cloud is final, then put it on the screen.
  • Keep it kind. Clouds meant to embarrass or hurt someone get removed, and anyone can ask for a cloud about them to be taken down.
