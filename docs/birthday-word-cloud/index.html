A birthday word cloud from everyone who loves them

Milestone birthdays deserve more than cake. Share one link with family and friends, everyone adds three words about the birthday person, and by the party there's a portrait no one person could have written.

The words people pick again and again grow largest, so the cloud shows what everyone agrees on, and the one-off words show the surprises.

Start one now — free, no app

Takes ten seconds. Nobody needs an account.

How it works

  1. Start it. Name it after the person and the occasion, like "Grandma Rose's 80th".
  2. Share the link in the family group chat, a message to old friends, a note in the party invitation.
  3. Everyone adds three words from their phone or computer. Answers are anonymous, and each person gets one set.
  4. Watch it grow. You can hide any word you don't want and close the cloud when you're done.

Words people often choose

generous, funny, kind, wise, warm, loving, adventurous, stubborn, fearless, joyful, loyal, devoted, mischievous, curious, strong, patient, creative, playful, caring, legendary, gracious, witty, brave, home

The best clouds mix the obvious with the specific. Encourage people to pick the words they'd actually use about this person, not the safest ones.

Tips

  • Send it two weeks to a few days before the party. Most answers come in on the first day, so one reminder is plenty.
  • Start it no more than a month ahead: clouds started on the website stop taking new words after 30 days.
  • For a child's birthday, only a parent or guardian should start the cloud.
  • Keep it kind. Clouds meant to embarrass or hurt someone get removed, and anyone can ask for a cloud about them to be taken down.
