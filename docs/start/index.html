three·words

Start a word cloud

For a birthday, a retirement, a send-off, or anything at all. Share one link, everyone adds three words, and the cloud grows live. Free, and nobody needs the app.

People will see: Describe … in three words

Keep it kind: a cloud meant to embarrass or hurt someone gets removed. Only people with the link can find it. The rules

How it works

  1. Name it: a person, an occasion, a team, a year.
  2. Share the link wherever your people are: a group chat, an email, a card.
  3. Everyone adds three words from their browser. Anonymous, no account.
  4. Watch it grow. Hide any word you don't want, and close it when you're done.

Prefer an app? three·words is free on iPhone and Android, and it can also ask the people who know you how they see you.
