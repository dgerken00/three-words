A farewell word cloud for someone who's moving on

A colleague's last day, a friend moving away, a teammate heading off to something new. Instead of passing a card around, share one link: everyone adds three words, and the cloud shows what they'll be remembered for.

It works for people who are far away too. Nobody needs an app or an account, so it reaches everyone a leaving card would miss.

Start one now — free, no app

Takes ten seconds. Nobody needs an account.

How it works

  1. Start it. Name it after the person and the occasion, like "Alex's send-off".
  2. Share the link in the team channel, the group chat, an email to everyone who should be in it.
  3. Everyone adds three words from their phone or computer. Answers are anonymous, and each person gets one set.
  4. Watch it grow. You can hide any word you don't want and close the cloud when you're done.

Words people often choose

brilliant, supportive, hilarious, kind, reliable, curious, brave, generous, creative, calm, honest, upbeat, thoughtful, legend, energetic, patient, clever, positive, genuine, inspiring, warm, adventurous, unforgettable, missed

The best clouds mix the obvious with the specific. Encourage people to pick the words they'd actually use about this person, not the safest ones.

Tips

  • Start it as soon as the news is out, so people can add words before the last day.
  • Share it where everyone already talks, and leave it open until the day itself.
  • On the last day, put the cloud on screen or send a screenshot to the group as the goodbye.
  • Keep it kind. Clouds meant to embarrass or hurt someone get removed, and anyone can ask for a cloud about them to be taken down.
