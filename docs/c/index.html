What is this?

A public experiment by three·words, a small app built around one question: how do the people who know you really see you? In the app, you invite your friends and family and each of them sends three words that describe you. People who use the app can also start a cloud about any subject, like this one, and share the link.

How it works. No account. Your three words are anonymous. One set per person (per browser — you can change yours, not add more). A filter blocks slurs before they're saved, and words that are cruel rather than honest can be hidden by the person who started the cloud. Beyond your three words, the only things stored are a random token your browser keeps so you can edit them and a hashed IP for rate-limiting — see the privacy policy.

Something wrong? A cloud must not target a private person or be hateful or abusive. Use "Report this cloud" under the cloud, or email admin@threewordsapp.com. Reported clouds are paused and reviewed by a person.
