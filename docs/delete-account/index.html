Delete your three·words account

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

Open three·words → tap Account → Delete my account. This immediately and permanently removes your profile and every word sent to or from you. No waiting.

Option 2 — By email (no app needed)

Don't have the app installed anymore? Email admin@threewordsapp.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account." We'll delete your account and data within 5 business days and confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, invite code, account credentials)
  • Every word you sent to others, and every word sent to you
  • Your reports and blocks

This cannot be undone. See our Privacy Policy for what we collect and why.

Questions? admin@threewordsapp.com
